# My Circular Garden Measurements

## Description
### This is program manipulates the math library in order to calculate the area, circumference, square root of area, area rounded down, and area rounded up

## Input Requirements
### - The *Radius* of a Circle [in *Meters*]

## How to Run the Code
### First place the radius of a circle
### The measurements will automatically pop up

# Sample Ouput

### Enter the radius of the circular garden in meters : 7

### The area of the circular garden with a radius of 7.0 meters is:  153.94 square meters. 

### The circumference of the circular garden with a radius of 7.0 meters is:  43.98 meters.

### The square root of the area of the circular garden with a radius of 7.0 is:  12.41 square meters. 

### The area rounded down with a radius of 7.0 meters is:  153 square meters.

### The area rounded up with a radius of 7.0 meters is:  154 square meters.
